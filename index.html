Destroy everything

Blow up the world. Make a precision cut with the laser, or throw a Hail Mary with the laser grenade and see what's still standing once the dust settles. There is no cover here, because nothing survives being shot at.

Armored cars don't have health bars. No repairs. If you want someone gone, you take the ground out from under them.

Bring friends to throw around

Up to four players, online or passing a single controller around the couch. Free-for-all, or split into teams. Short a player? Hand the seat to a CPU.

Make a car fly across the map. Yours or the enemy's. Some turns you launch a rival into the void. Some turns you launch your teammate. Some turns you launch yourself, and everyone gets to watch it happen.

Take on the machines

Nobody around to play? The CPUs are always up for a match. Pick a gentle sparring partner while you learn the ropes, or one that spots the shot you missed and sends you into the void with it.

Scenarios stack the odds against you: a lone car against a whole squad, or you and a friend against an army. Play them alone, or bring a friend along on the couch or online.

Share your maps

Sculpt levels in the in-game editor and drop straight into a match on them without ever leaving the game. Import your own images or draw from scratch, then add lights and spawn points.

Publish the ones worth keeping to the Steam Workshop, where new arenas are one click away.

Hone your skills

Speeders wanted! Solo challenges are the hardest driving test in the game: knock every enemy car off the map as fast as you can. Finding a solution is only half the job. The other half is perfecting the execution.

Every run is scored on the global leaderboard, so there's always a name sitting one row above yours. Or skip the scoring entirely: sandbox mode hands you unlimited weapons and an empty arena to take apart however you like.
